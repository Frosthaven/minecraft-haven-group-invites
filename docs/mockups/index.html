haven-group-invites: screen mockups

Renders of the planned screens, drawn at about Minecraft GUI scale 3 over the blurred world, in the vanilla Social Interactions style. Player names and skins are placeholders. A group works like a party invite in an MMO: you invite a player, they accept, and everyone in your group gets the same permissions in your claims.

How this maps to FTB: the mod keeps the group list on the server. For each group member with a party, every other group member becomes an FTB ally of that party. A checked box sets that FTB Chunks setting to allies, an unchecked box sets it to private. Settings belong to the party, so only its owner can change them.
